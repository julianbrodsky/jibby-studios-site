Captain Contract

Sit ye down, and I'll tell ye of the summer I sold cover along the coast.

I had no ship worth the name, only a hat and a ledger. But every harbor had someone frettin'. A sailor feared his crew wouldn't come back for him. A grocer had promised sweet melons and wasn't sure of a single one. A crab had a feast ridin' on the Shell Cup. Each told me how often things went wrong and what it would cost if they did, and each asked the same question: will ye cover me?

Some paid a fair price. Some were bargains, if ye did the sums. And some were traps that looked safe right up until the tide came in. I wrote the good ones, turned down the rest, and never put too much of my purse on any one promise.

Thirty-four places I walked that year, from mushroom forests to glacier caves to a lantern festival over the water. By the end I had a ship, a crew of ten, and an appointment with the Oracle.

Captain Contract

Ah, a visitor. Pull up a barrel. The fire's warm and the night is long, and I've a tale ye haven't heard.

When I was a boy, no taller than a mop, I signed aboard a leaky sloop called the Persimmon. Her captain was an old woman named Mags who wore three coats at once and never raised her voice. She didn't chase gold. She chased weather. Where a storm was brewin', there went Mags, and there went the rest of us, bailing.

One spring we rode a gale for nine days. On the tenth the clouds tore open, and there in the calm sat an island nobody had charted, round as a biscuit, with one tree on it and a goat beneath the tree. The goat looked at us. We looked at the goat. Mags rowed ashore alone and was gone till sundown.

When she came back she was carrying a lantern I'd never seen before, and she wouldn't say a word about where she got it. That lantern never once went out, not in rain, not in wind, not when Fergus the cook sat on it. We sailed by its light for eleven more years.

I asked her about the island on the day she gave me the Persimmon. She only laughed and said some maps are drawn in ink and some are drawn in nerve. I've been looking for that goat ever since.

The lantern's on the shelf behind ye. Mind the goat.
