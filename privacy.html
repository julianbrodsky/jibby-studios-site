Captain Contract

An offline iOS game about a pirate who runs an insurance agency and decides which policies are worth writing.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026.

Captain Contract collects no data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and never connects to the internet.

Your progress and settings (sound and vibration) are stored only on your device. They are included in your own iCloud or computer backups of the device if you use those, and are deleted when you delete the app. To erase your progress without deleting the app, use Settings, then Restart run, inside the game.

No data is shared with any third party, because no data leaves your device. The game asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications.

Coins in the game are pretend. They cannot be bought, sold or cashed out for anything real.
